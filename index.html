Welcome and Rules
Hi, I'm just starting out.
  • Please contact me :) My turnaround time is usually somewhere between an hour and a week. Feel free to send a friendly reminder if I haven't replied after a few days.
  • If you find something you want, please send your full list. But I'd be happy to take any recommendations with it.
  • I provide links via MEGA and accept MEGA and Google Drive for receiving.
  • My links will work for at least a week. Didn't manage in time? Just ask for a fresh link!

General Info & Stats

  • Use YYYY-MM-DD when seaching dates. For unknown day/month use "00". Search 2026-10-00 for undated "Oct 2026" recordings, or 2026-00-00 for recordings without a published month and day.
  • I am abbreviating a lot due to lack of space, but seachablity is still there. You can search "February", "Evening", "Revival" and "First National Tour", even though I list them as "Feb", "E", "Rev." and "1NT"
  • Painful as it is, city names use their English spelling (e.g., Cologne, not Köln)
  • Adaptations of English shows are listed under their English title.
  • Entries are sorted alphabetically by title (ignoring leading articles), then by date (newest to oldest)
  • Subtitles are not hardcoded (can be toggled on or off) - unless stated otherwise
  • My firsts: 2026-10-18 2026-10-19 2026-10-21
